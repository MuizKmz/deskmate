DeskMate voice pack
===================

A small offline voice for DeskMate (https://github.com/MuizKmz/deskmate). DeskMate downloads
this folder once into %APPDATA%\DeskMate\voicepack and starts DeskMate-Voice.exe by itself when
she speaks. It only listens on 127.0.0.1 (this PC) and stops when DeskMate quits.

This pack is a separate program from DeskMate.exe. Because it includes phonemizer and
espeak-ng (both GNU GPL version 3), the pack as a whole is distributed under the GNU General
Public License, version 3 or later. You can get its complete source code from the links below;
the full license texts are in the licenses folder.
